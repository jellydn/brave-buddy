External Singapore legal counsel and a child-safeguarding professional must approve their areas. ## Prepared response sequence

1. **Receive privately:** Use the approved private channel. Do not copy child data into public issues, pull requests, chat, or logs.
2. **Protect:** If authorized, contain exposure without deleting evidence that the approved policy requires. Do not investigate a child's disclosure in the product.
3. **Classify:** Security, privacy, and safeguarding owners decide impact and urgency within their approved scopes.
4. **Escalate:** Follow the reviewed regional matrix for imminent danger, sexual abuse, serious abuse, self-harm, or legal notification. This repository does not define that matrix.
5. **Remediate and verify:** Link fixes to a private incident record, run regression checks, and obtain owner approval before release.
6. **Notify and disclose:** Only authorized owners decide caregiver, regulator, provider, or public communication.
7. **Learn:** Record non-sensitive corrective actions, control gaps, and owners without publishing child or incident details.

## Current prototype constraint

If an incident requires a pending decision in `docs/LAUNCH_READINESS_REGISTER.md`, stop release work and escalate to Dung and the required external reviewer. Automated checks and repository contributors cannot approve the response.
