# Scenario Coverage Audit

**Audit date:** 2026-09-10
**Catalog:** `src/content/scenarios.ts` at 10 draft scenarios

This audit describes the current human-authored catalog. It is not expert approval. Counts must be updated when scenarios are added, withdrawn, or reclassified.

## Current coverage

| Dimension | Current evidence | Gap or gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Learning worlds | 2 scenarios in each of Making Friends, Friendship Problems, Teasing, Bullying, and Asking for Help | Quantity is below the planned 30–50 scenarios. |
| Situation types | 2 misunderstanding, 1 ordinary conflict, 2 teasing, 1 repeated bullying, and 4 unsafe behavior | Ordinary conflict and repeated bullying each have only one example. |
| Settings | Birthday Party, Canteen, Classroom, Home, Online World, Playground, School Bus, and Sports Club | All planned MVP settings appear, but most have one example. |
| Voice or accent shaming | `teasing-voice` | Needs cultural, accessibility, and safeguarding review. |
| Appearance shaming | `teasing-appearance-upstander` | Needs cultural, accessibility, and safeguarding review. |
| Exclusion | `friends-party-upstander` and `bullying-repeated-exclusion` | Includes positive inclusion and repeated targeted harm. |
| Peer pressure | `problems-pressure-dare` | Only an unsafe physical dare is covered. Everyday social pressure remains a gap. |
| Digital safety | `help-online-secret` | Broader online friendship, group-chat exclusion, and pressure remain gaps. |
| Upstander behavior | `friends-party-upstander` and `teasing-appearance-upstander` | Add reviewed examples for repeated bullying and online settings. |
| Positive friendship building | 2 Making Friends scenarios | Expand before the product grows so difficult situations do not dominate. |
| Trusted-adult escalation | 5 scenarios require adult help | Wording and regional routes need qualified safeguarding review. |
| Age adaptations | Every scene and response has text for ages 6–8 and 9–12 | Reading load and age suitability need testing with children and accessibility specialists. |
| Skill themes | Courage 9, Kindness 3, Calmness 3, Friendship 3, Safety 7 | Courage and Safety dominate; future reviewed content should broaden the other themes. |
| Editorial state | All 10 scenarios are version 1 and `draft` | No scenario has named expert approval or release-owner publication evidence. |

## Safe next content work

1. Keep new content human-authored and in `draft` state.
2. Prioritize positive friendship, ordinary conflict, Calmness, Kindness, and Friendship coverage.
3. Add no scenario to `approved` or `published` until the named reviewers in `docs/CONTENT_WORKFLOW.md` provide evidence.
4. Select readability targets only with the accessibility/content reviewer. Do not treat automated word or sentence counts as age suitability.
5. Re-run this audit before each planned content expansion milestone.

## Evidence limits

The catalog and validation tests prove structure and current range. They do not prove that wording is clinically sound, culturally suitable, accessible, legally compliant, or safe for public launch.
